# Data reuse and source rights

Calgary Neighbourhood Analytics is an independent, noncommercial research project. These notes describe the permissions and limits used for the current public release, reviewed on 14 September 2026. They are not blanket legal clearance. Permission to reuse data is separate from its accuracy, privacy implications and suitability for a particular decision.

The current scope excludes paid access, advertising, brokerage promotion, affiliate commissions and lead collection. Some sources allow commercial reuse; others do not. Recheck those terms before changing how the project is used. A free website can still serve a commercial purpose.

The [source reuse register](data/rights-register.json) records individual datasets and licence links. [Third-party notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt) cover software and map design. Keep source dates, coverage notes, credits and licence links with redistributed data and reports.

## City data and public property records

The [City of Calgary Open Government Licence](https://data.calgary.ca/stories/s/Open-Calgary-Terms-of-Use/u45n-7awa/) permits reuse, changes and distribution of covered information, including commercially, with attribution and its other conditions. Personal information, rights the City cannot license and official symbols are excluded. Do not imply City endorsement.

The app uses expressly designated open datasets for assessments, boundaries, transit, census context, development and infrastructure. For example, the metadata for [assessments](https://data.calgary.ca/api/views/4bsw-nn7w.json) and [public water services](https://data.calgary.ca/api/views/ta76-7bfx.json) identifies the licence. Ordinary [Calgary.ca website terms](https://www.calgary.ca/info-requests/terms-of-use.html) are more restrictive; they do not give the same permission to republish webpages, PDFs or compilations. Use the designated data services rather than expanding into webpage scraping.

Property records are limited to parcel/account facts such as civic address, assessment, roll number, year built and public infrastructure. The app does not publish owner names, occupants, personal contact details, private tax bills or tenant records. An address can still become personal information in context; an open-data label does not resolve that question. See the Alberta OIPC's [assessment-address analysis](https://oipc.ab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Order-F2016-51.pdf). Do not combine these datasets to identify or profile residents.

Ward and community fallbacks are calculated from [open community polygons](https://data.calgary.ca/d/surr-xmvs) and [open ward data](https://data.calgary.ca/d/tz8z-hyaz), rather than a copied webpage list. Split coverage stays uncertain until an address resolves it. Area overlaps are not population shares or an official ward/community assignment. The [open quadrant dataset](https://data.calgary.ca/d/g2n2-qnvh) supplies quadrant geometry; its licence does not cover every similarly named ArcGIS item.

## Historical crime and census coverage

Bundled crime figures come from the City-open [2018–2019 Community Crime and Disorder Statistics](https://data.calgary.ca/d/848s-4m4z). They cover selected historical categories, not current safety or a complete crime total. Downloading them recently does not make them current.

The [current CPS workbook](https://www.calgarypolice.ca/transparency-and-accountability/crime-statistics.html) remains link-only. Community-profile PDF extracts whose redistribution rights were not established are also excluded. A personal or unpaid project does not itself supply permission to republish them. Census context instead uses the City's open [population and age](https://data.calgary.ca/d/f9wk-wej9) and [household-size](https://data.calgary.ca/d/msjx-5ygv) datasets; unavailable income, tenure and shelter-cost measures remain unavailable. Any future directly sourced Statistics Canada material needs its own [licence check](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/terms-conditions/open-licence), including the restriction on linking information to identify people, businesses or organizations.

## Maps, aerial imagery and attribution

The City's [orthophoto guide](https://files-us-prod.cms.commerce.dynamics.com/cms/api/tsbgmhdjmt/binary/MA4zyF), dated 8 January 2026, identifies standard hosted imagery for public web-map viewing, including `Calgary_Orthophoto_Web_2025`. The app requests that service directly and credits © The City of Calgary, 2025. It does not bundle, proxy or include aerial imagery in exported research briefs.

That is a **direct-viewing permission**, not an unrestricted image licence. The [ArcGIS item](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=cd804df9f1b747108dad79d0286cce3d) reserves reproduction rights, and the [download product](https://cityonline.calgary.ca/orthophotos-2025/5637150576.p) restricts republication. The guide also treats Pictometry separately. Do not use aerial screenshots in social posts, preview images or downloadable reports on the strength of the viewing permission. Share the website link or an appropriately credited vector-map view instead.

[OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/) allows website/app integration under its [service terms](https://openfreemap.org/tos/). Normal map delivery is distinct from bulk tile collection. Keep linked OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap credits visible on the map. The building layer uses vector geometry, not Google photorealistic imagery.

The normalized [core OSM amenity extract](data/access-amenities-osm.geojson) and [supplemental extract](data/access-supplement-osm.geojson) remain separately downloadable with their source metadata. Retain that database offer, attribution and applicable [ODbL conditions](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), including share-alike where required. This does not automatically relicense independent City datasets or all app code. Keep [OSM attribution](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines) and working links to both extracts in printed/exported results. City source credits should travel with reports even when mobility data is unavailable. Generate public examples from the live website so links work outside a development machine.

The map adapts Positron through OpenMapTiles/OpenFreeMap, changes colours and adds thematic layers. Preserve the CARTO, Stamen and Paul Norman design credits, the change description, and the applicable [Positron code/design licences](https://github.com/openmaptiles/positron-gl-style/blob/master/LICENSE.md). The [OpenFreeMap style inventory](https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap-styles/blob/main/LICENSE.md) also identifies font and sprite licences. These credits are separate from the underlying map-data licence.

## Flood and air information

The provincial flood layer's [exact metadata](https://geodiscover.alberta.ca/geoportal/rest/metadata/item/9b49d5d5267a4ae0bb936861bb70cce3/html) and [federal catalogue record](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/d192b9b1-caac-405e-8d2a-ba6b21929c3f) identify Alberta's open licence. [Open Government Licence – Alberta, version 2.2](https://open.alberta.ca/licence), permits reuse and modification with acknowledgement, exclusions and no implied endorsement. It excludes personal information, unlicensed third-party rights and official symbols. Keep its attribution and the disclosed clipping, projection and simplification with the GeoJSON. A generalized polygon is not a parcel-level flood, engineering or insurance determination.

ECCC AQHI data uses the [ECCC Data Services End-use Licence](https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/licence/readme_en/). Credit Environment and Climate Change Canada and any identified third-party originators, link the licence, and preserve observation times. The licence's restrictions on changing weather-alert content or intent and on official marks still apply. Regional AQHI and station PM2.5 observations do not establish exposure at an individual home.

## Representatives and election results

The House of Commons [Open Data page](https://www.ourcommons.ca/en/open-data) expressly supplies the [current-members XML](https://www.ourcommons.ca/Members/en/search/xml). The app uses limited factual district, officeholder and caucus fields with source dates. This permission does not extend to every biography, portrait, logo or parliamentary recording. Keep current affiliation separate from election-time affiliation.

**Current MLAs are link-only.** Provincial entries identify geographic districts and link to the [official Assembly directory](https://www.assembly.ab.ca/members/members-of-the-legislative-assembly); they do not reproduce its current names, affiliations, member profiles or vacancy labels. The Assembly's [permitted-use terms](https://www.assembly.ab.ca/disclaimer) contain an unresolved election-period restriction. Linking avoids relying on a broad interpretation of that permission. Independently sourced district geometry and historical election results have their own terms. Do not infer an officeholder from an unconfirmed election outcome.

[Elections Canada](https://www.elections.ca/content.aspx?document=index&lang=e&section=pri) permits qualifying noncommercial reproduction with accuracy and source acknowledgement. Keep complete report/table titles, authors where available, original result URLs and the unofficial-reproduction statement beside the figures. Keep the [original boundary publication](https://www.elections.ca/content.aspx?dir=cir/mapsCorner/vector&document=index&lang=e&section=res) identified. Independent calculations must remain distinct from reproduced results.

[Elections Alberta's terms](https://www.elections.ab.ca/terms-conditions/) allow qualifying noncommercial educational reproduction with attribution, accuracy and no implied endorsement, but prohibit modifying reproduced material. Preserve published votes, shares and turnout; label calculated margins as app analysis. Provincial geometry retains source vertices and attributes after projection/format conversion. The publisher's [GIS downloads](https://www.elections.ab.ca/resources/maps/) support use in mapping software, but this technical-conversion interpretation is not an unrestricted adaptation grant. Seek confirmation or link to the official map before making changes beyond that basis. Neither election source grants permission to use official crests, party logos or portraits merely because a related factual result is reproduced.

## Calculations, software and future releases

Financial tools use limited sourced numeric rules and independent calculations, rather than copied official calculator code, tables or PDFs. [CIPO's copyright guide](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/guide-copyright) distinguishes facts from protected expression; it does not remove website-contract restrictions or authorize copying entire compilations. [CMHC](https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/about-us/terms-conditions), [Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/transparency/terms.html), [Alberta.ca](https://www.alberta.ca/disclaimer) and Calgary.ca have separate terms. Keep rate dates and source links, and reassess before adding automated collection from those webpages.

Keep assessments distinct from sale prices, tax scenarios from adopted bills, and mortgage illustrations from lender approval. Public water records do not verify private plumbing; access estimates are not proprietary Walk Score/Transit Score data or verified walking routes. General information is not client representation or a licence to offer regulated services. Brokerage promotion, negotiation or personalized professional services would require a separate review under the relevant [RECA rules](https://www.reca.ca/rules/). Clear qualifications must appear beside claims, since fine print cannot repair a misleading [overall impression](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices-digest-volume-1).

Keep the complete software notices, the copied MapLibre worker/shared modules' [licence](vendor/maplibre/LICENSE.txt), and the vendored [shadcn licence](../vendor/shadcn-tailwind-4.13.0.LICENSE.md). Original-code licensing does not replace those terms or the separate data licences. The notices include some development/optional packages; inclusion does not mean every package runs in the browser. Some notices identify an upstream supplement where a package omitted its notice; preserve those qualifications rather than claiming all notices were release-pinned. Recheck notices when dependencies or distributed bundles change, particularly before distributing installed native binaries or a container. Do not copy restricted research files, credentials or private hosting configuration into a public release.

The live site's `/privacy` page and the [privacy operations guide](../docs/privacy-operations.md) explain browser storage, requests, provider processing and manual duties. Data reuse permission does not replace those duties. Hosting/map providers can process technical information outside Canada, and Vercel Hobby processor-contract coverage remains unverified where such coverage is required.

Before releasing a new snapshot, review its source terms, dates, coverage, attribution and sensitive fields together. Keep the data manifest and visible explanations aligned with the actual files. New commercial uses, sources or imagery exports need a fresh permission assessment. Neither these notes nor the project name assert official endorsement, trademark clearance or universal legal compliance.
