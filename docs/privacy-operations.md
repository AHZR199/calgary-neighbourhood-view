# Privacy operations — Calgary Neighbourhood View

Reviewed 14 September 2026; feature-processing inventory updated 16 September 2026. Operator: Abdullah Zubair, Alberta, Canada. Public contact: az28140@icloud.com. This guide accompanies the live site's `/privacy` page. It describes how the app handles information and what the operator needs to do. It is not a certification of worldwide compliance.

## How the app handles information

- A public, server-rendered privacy notice with contact, data categories, purposes, providers, international processing, retention, choices, rights, complaints and effective date. It is linked from Explore, Saved, Sources and About. Opening `/privacy` directly does not mount the map or run property searches.
- Optional local browser storage for saved places and checklists, with a shared deletion control. Clearing is reflected in other open tabs. Deletion is limited to the app's saved-research keys. It cannot erase other devices, exported files, emails or provider records.
- Address searches and property-detail requests use bounded JSON POST bodies. Browser and server responses use `no-store`; upstream fetches also opt out of Next caching. Compatible GET endpoints remain, but responses also use `no-store`. POST reduces URL logging; it does not make request processing anonymous or prevent infrastructure from inspecting bodies.
- No-referrer HTTP policy and HTML metadata reduce referral disclosure, including from shared address links. Sharing deliberately creates an address-bearing URL; visiting it still exposes that URL to the host and can leave history or recipient copies.
- The app does not include visitor accounts, lead collection, advertising integrations, a visitor analytics SDK, session replay, marketing cookies, browser geolocation, a search-history database or a custom log drain. Providers still carry out their own delivery, security and other processing described in their policies.

In browsers supporting `document.modelContext`, the app registers tools that can read selected place/property identifiers and comparison IDs or change selections when invoked. Tool results can include a public property address. The public policy explains this optional browser/assistant integration. Browser and assistant providers apply their own permissions and practices; the integration does not create an app-owned remote user database.

## Duties for the operator

1. Monitor az28140@icloud.com, including spam, and check that enquiries can reach it. Keep the mailbox available without a social-media login requirement.
2. Use unique credentials, multi-factor authentication and limited account/team access for Vercel and Apple. Review account access and retention settings regularly. Do not upload identity documents, emails, production credentials or visitor logs to the public source repository.
3. Log requests privately with receipt date, type, applicable jurisdiction, response deadline, verification performed, action and deletion/review date. Use the minimum identifying information. Do not create a public request log.
4. Acknowledge promptly, aim to resolve ordinary access/correction/deletion requests within 30 days and obey any shorter applicable limit. GDPR uses **one calendar month**; PIPEDA ordinarily 30 days; Alberta PIPA ordinarily 45 days. If CCPA applies, acknowledge covered requests within 10 business days, ordinarily respond within 45 calendar days, and handle applicable opt-outs within 15 business days. Check the relevant law before an extension, fee or refusal. Explain reasons, review/appeal routes and regulators on time.
5. Verify proportionately; do not routinely demand government ID for a simple query. Accept properly authorized agents. A browser-only shortlist may need a user-controlled export or screenshot voluntarily supplied; do not collect extra identifiers merely to link an otherwise unlinked visit.
6. Search only operator-controlled information relevant to the request. Contact a provider where necessary and legally required; distinguish records you control from its independent processing. Explain limits honestly. Correct a displayed public fact where justified without pretending the City source was also changed.
7. Record resolution and perform the 12-month correspondence deletion review manually. Delete earlier when no continuing purpose exists and a valid deletion request requires it. Review exceptional holds, rather than retaining all enquiries indefinitely.
8. Before adding analytics, leads, ads, accounts, monetization, new providers, precise location or EU-directed services, recheck legal scope, provider contracts, consent needs and retention; update the public notice before the new use. Publish a conspicuous dated change notice where material. A banner that asserts consent without choice would not solve these duties.

## Legal scope and remaining limitations

This is a free, independent Alberta-operated research project. That does not establish exemption from all privacy laws. Alberta PIPA and PIPEDA depend on actual activity; brokerage promotion or lead generation could change the commercial analysis. GDPR depends on establishment, EU targeting or monitoring, rather than worldwide website accessibility alone. US state laws vary, and CalOPPA is distinct from CCPA thresholds. Reassess applicable duties, including any registration or representative requirements, when the operation, audience or processing changes.

**Vercel Hobby contract limitation:** The current published DPA defines customer processing coverage for Pro/Enterprise. This demo is on Hobby; verified Article 28 processor-contract or SCC coverage for Hobby has not been established. Before relying on GDPR compliance where GDPR applies, determine each provider's actual role, obtain applicable contractual coverage and transfer mechanisms, or choose a provider/configuration with verified terms. Keep this limitation clear in the public policy until it is resolved.

Known locations: the current Vercel functions deploy to `iad1`, United States; edge, support and subprocessor locations can vary. OpenFreeMap's operator is Hungarian, but its terms do not establish Hungary-only hosting and it may use Cloudflare. Cloudflare describes primarily US/EEA storage and global access. Esri and Apple describe US/international processing. A complete provider country inventory and any required transfer-impact assessment remain operating tasks. If Alberta overseas-provider duties or GDPR transfer rules apply, complete that work and the required safeguards. A general international-processing disclosure does not replace them.

## Preliminary legitimate-interests assessment

This is a starting assessment for purposes that may rely on GDPR Article 6(1)(f). It does not establish a basis for every provider's independent use or meet the separate conditions for sensitive information.

- **Purpose:** Deliver specifically requested public-property/map results, protect the service, publish relevant civic context and answer privacy enquiries. These are concrete research/service interests, not behavioural advertising or financial eligibility decisions.
- **Necessity:** IP transport and narrow search fields are needed for network delivery/querying. Selected browser storage is needed for the requested persistent shortlist/checklist. An account, tracking identifier, owner profile, continuous GPS, persistent search log and marketing analytics are unnecessary and omitted. POST/no-referrer/no-store reduce unnecessary disclosure. Provider-specific retention still requires scrutiny.
- **Balancing:** Users expect a public research tool and optional device saves, but an address plus identity/IP can reveal a personal interest. A shared browser or address link can expose that interest. Protections include optional storage, deletion, no lead profiling, public-record limits, explicit provider notice and accessible objections. The host/map contract and retention gaps above remain; reassess or change providers if the impact cannot be justified under applicable law.
- **Named officials and political affiliation:** Public civic facts are limited to public roles and attributed official/election sources. This does not justify inferring residents' beliefs. If GDPR governs processing about identifiable individuals, separately assess Article 9 for political information, including evidence that the relevant person manifestly made it public where that condition is relied on, and Article 14 notice duties/exceptions. An open-data licence alone establishes neither. No blanket Article 9/14 clearance is asserted.
- **Consent:** Continued browsing alone is not treated as consent. Optional storage responds to the user's explicit feature action. Assess applicable ePrivacy/device-storage rules before new storage or tracking; seek valid consent wherever required. Rely on a legal obligation only when there is an actual applicable duty.

## Incident procedure

Contain an actual exposure, preserve only necessary evidence privately, revoke affected credentials and contact the relevant provider. Record when the incident was discovered, affected data/people, likely harm, containment and decisions. Assess applicable reporting promptly: Alberta PIPA requires commissioner reporting for a real risk of significant harm; PIPEDA has its own significant-harm notification/reporting and breach-record duties; GDPR generally requires supervisory reporting within 72 hours of awareness unless unlikely to risk rights/freedoms, with separate high-risk individual notification. US breach rules vary by affected residents and data type. Follow legal requirements even where fewer records or no accounts are involved. Document notification decisions and send required notices within the applicable deadlines.

## 16 September 2026 feature changes

Sun calculations run on the visitor's device using the selected property's public coordinates. Date, time and facing choices stay in page memory, with no new storage, geolocation permission, API or processor. Regional radon context is a bundled historical extract with postal identifiers omitted; visitors cannot submit individual test results. Construction years use the existing City lookup. Sale records remain unavailable, with ordinary external links to official services; no authenticated accounts or purchased records are accessed.

The public privacy notice and /terms describe these distinctions. Keep them in sync if adding solar imagery, shading services, saved solar preferences, radon submissions or a licensed sales-data provider later. Existing provider-contract and international-processing limitations remain unchanged.

Bus, CTrain and planned-route overlay choices stay in browser memory. Their geometry is bundled with the app and served by the existing host. No device location, trip-planner API, live-vehicle service or new processor is introduced. External official transit links remain governed by those services when clicked.

School filters, query text and distances also stay in browser memory. The City/Alberta school derivative is served by the existing host; school links do not prefill the selected home or submit student data. Map clicks send the selected public point to `/api/map-property` in a POST body, with no-store responses. The server queries the same public City assessment service as address search; its upstream Socrata URL contains the spatial filter. No app logging, persistent search database, geolocation permission or new processor is added. These are selected research locations, not device-location readings. The privacy notice distinguishes these flows.

The neighbourhood finder fetches one bundled public profile file from the existing host and calculates matches locally. Budget amounts, quadrant selections, distance limits, selected school group, priorities and results are temporary page state. They are not written to localStorage, appended to URLs, uploaded to a recommendation endpoint or sent to an AI service. There is no visitor-preference database, new processor or new tracking. Opening a matched neighbourhood or comparing its code uses the existing app features; separately saving a place still follows the existing explicit browser-save action. The model uses public location/service and assessment context, not demographic, crime, political or resident-profile criteria.

The nine landmark illustrations—Calgary Tower, Saddledome, Bow, Peace Bridge, Wonderland, TELUS Sky, Central Library, Historic City Hall with Municipal Building context, and Canada Olympic Park—are generated locally from bundled original Three.js code in one shared renderer. Their minimal public footprint and companion-building extracts are served by the existing host. They fetch no external model, image texture or model-viewer service and collect no extra information. Selecting a landmark only changes the local map view and details. Existing hosting and basemap requests still occur; there is no new processor or tracking. Review these statements before adding saved finder preferences, shared preference URLs, recommendation telemetry, external models or server-side recommendation services.
